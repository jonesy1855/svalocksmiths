Who Am I

EX-FORCES PROFESSIONAL

Built on Military Discipline & Technical Precision

Following a career in the armed forces, I established Shropshire Vehicle Access Locksmiths to bring a disciplined, structured approach to the mobile auto-locksmith trade across Shrewsbury, Telford, and Shropshire.

Every auto-locksmith has a starting point. For me, it began right here on the dining room table, creating a workaround so that I could use the AK90+ to decode and then re-code my own new key and writing transponder data directly to the module (this was the BMW/Mini R53 EWS systems). From this my passion grew.

Whether decoding a highly secure vehicle module on my workspace bench or picking open a locked commercial van on a wet roadside, we approach every task with the exact technical accuracy required to secure your vehicle safely.
