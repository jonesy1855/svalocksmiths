Built for the Field

The free web Lishi Finder on this site already covers the full vehicle catalogue — cars, vans, HGV, farm machinery, bikes & ATVs and newer models — but it needs a connection. SVA FieldMate Pro (formerly the SVA Lishi Picker) ships the same database offline, so you get the right tool identification even in dead-signal areas, underground car parks, or remote rural jobs — and Pro users get full job intelligence on the same card.

  • Fully Offline Finder — Free Make/model/year lookup for Lishi tools, Silca blanks, Instacode and transponders works without any internet connection — the database ships inside the app.
  • Pro Job Intelligence Module locations & trim access tips, risk guides, ballpark job pricing, vehicle A/C specs, AKL triage and offline van notes — unlocked with Pro.
  • Job Quotes & Invoices (Pro) Build quotes, then generate and print PDF invoices directly from your phone — ready to send before you leave the job.
  • Pro Unlock £7.99 subscription, automatically renews for full unlimited access — sign up via Google Play and it unlocks inside the app. The free tier stays free.
