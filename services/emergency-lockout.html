What is "Non-Destructive" Entry?

Gaining access to your vehicle without causing physical damage to the lock cylinder, bodywork, or glass. We strictly avoid crude force, pry bars, or generic wire hangers that ruin weather seals.

How We Work: Technical Precision

Instead of forcing the door, we decode and pick the lock mechanically using precision diagnostic tools (Lishi picks).

By inserting these specialist picks directly into the keyway, we manipulate the internal wafers one by one — exactly as a real laser-cut key would. Once all wafers align, the lock rotates cleanly, leaving the mechanism in perfect working order.

Note on Deadlocked Vehicles Modern cars and commercial vans use "double-locking". Pulling interior handles or pushing dashboard buttons will not unlock it. Mechanical picking via the driver's door is the only safe way to bypass a deadlock state.
