Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Data Controller & Information We Collect

Shropshire Vehicle Access ("SVA Locksmiths", "we", "our", "us") operates as a mobile automotive locksmith service provider across Shropshire. For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, we act as the Data Controller.

When you contact us via website forms, telephone calls, or direct messaging channels (including WhatsApp), we collect and process:

  • Contact details: Full name, telephone number, and email address.
  • Vehicle identifiers: Vehicle Registration Mark (VRM), Make, Model, and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
  • Geographical data: Breakdown location, driveway address, or billing location required to coordinate our mobile workshop.
  • Identity verification data: Sight inspection of valid Photographic ID and matching V5C logbook documentation (conducted solely on-site to verify lawful vehicle connection; we do not store photocopies of personal identification cards unless required by law).

2. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your personal information under the following lawful bases set out in UK GDPR:

  • Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)): To calculate quotes, coordinate mobile workshop dispatch, cut physical keys, and perform vehicle security coding.
  • Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): To maintain statutory accounting records and sales invoices in accordance with HMRC auditing standards.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): To establish lawful entitlement to vehicle access and prevent automotive crime or unauthorised entry.

3. Data Processors & Third-Party Platforms

We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party marketing companies. Personal data is handled exclusively through our operational processors:

  • Website Form Processing: Inquiries submitted through web contact forms are transmitted securely via Formspree and stored within our protected Google Workspace infrastructure.
  • Direct Messaging: Chats initiated via our direct WhatsApp widget are governed by WhatsApp/Meta end-to-end encryption protocols.
  • Card Processing: In-person card payments are processed on-site through secure, PCI-DSS-compliant mobile point-of-sale terminals.

4. Data Retention & Your Statutory Rights

Invoicing, billing, and transactional dispatch records are retained for up to 6 years following the end of the relevant financial year to comply with HMRC compliance standards. General inquiries that do not proceed to a confirmed roadside callout are purged from our systems within 6 months.

Under UK GDPR, you retain the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data, or to restrict its processing. To submit a data request, contact us directly at info@sva-locksmiths.co.uk. You also hold the statutory right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

5. Cookies & Third-Party Media

Our website utilises essential session cookies to support site security and secure form submission. Embedded third-party elements (such as Google Maps for local routing and YouTube video demonstrations) may deploy functional or analytical cookies once consented to via our cookie banner. You may update or revoke cookie preferences at any time via your web browser settings.

Terms of Service

Standard Terms for Mobile Roadside Operations

1. Proof of Ownership & Right to Refuse

By requesting emergency roadside dispatch or booking locksmith services, you warrant that you are the lawful owner, registered keeper, or authorised fleet custodian of the target vehicle. We enforce a strict verification policy. Before any lock manipulation, picking, or transponder coding commences, our technicians will inspect a valid Photographic ID (Driving Licence or Passport) alongside a matching V5C logbook, current motor insurance schedule, or formal commercial fleet authorisation. We reserve the absolute right to terminate callouts, notify local law enforcement, and charge full callout rates if lawful authority cannot be established.

2. Emergency Roadside Dispatch & Cancellation Policy

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, by booking an immediate roadside intervention, you expressly request that our services commence immediately upon dispatch. You acknowledge that once our mobile workshop vehicle has mobilised and departed toward your location, standard callout charges remain fully payable should you cancel the request or fail to be present at the vehicle.

3. Non-Destructive Entry & Mechanical Pre-Existing Defects

Our vehicle access techniques utilise non-destructive manipulation (including Genuine Lishi decoding picks) designed to leave bodywork and locks undamaged. Shropshire Vehicle Access accepts no liability for pre-existing internal component failures, including seized wafer gates, fatigued central locking actuators, deadlocked latch breakdowns, or severed linkage rods present prior to arrival, nor for vehicle battery exhaustion preventing central locking systems from cycling.

4. Customer-Supplied Keys & Aftermarket Parts

Customers may occasionally supply their own replacement remote keys, transponder chips, or aftermarket blades. In such cases, cutting and programming fees remain payable in full regardless of whether third-party hardware successfully accepts coding or synchronises to the immobiliser. We provide zero warranty on customer-supplied parts and accept no liability for internal electronic or firmware defects present within third-party hardware.

5. SVA Hardware Warranties

All brand-new replacement keys, transponders, and remote fobs supplied and programmed directly by Shropshire Vehicle Access carry a 12-month warranty covering internal electronic manufacturing defects. This warranty is voided in cases of physical casing trauma, water damage, blade breakage from external force, unauthorised internal PCB modification, or standard exhaustion of coin-cell remote batteries.

6. Payment Terms

Payment is due strictly upon completion of the service on-site. We accept major credit and debit cards, contactless payments, and verified direct bank transfers. Repaired vehicles and cut keys will not be released until invoice settlement has been finalised. We reserve the right to request an upfront booking deposit or part-payment prior to dispatch for advance appointments, long-distance callouts, or specialised key orders.
