Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Data Controller & Collection

Shropshire Vehicle Access ("we", "our", "us") operates as a mobile auto locksmith service provider. For the purposes of UK GDPR regulations, we act as the data controller. When you submit an inquiry via our website forms or direct messaging (including WhatsApp), we collect your Name, Contact Details (Phone/Email), Vehicle Registration/VIN, and Location Data. This is processed strictly under the lawful basis of contractual necessity to coordinate our mobile workshop.

2. Data Processor & Storage

Your data is securely routed via our form architecture processor, Formspree, and lands directly into our secure Google Workspace environment. Communications initiated via our direct chat widget are facilitated by WhatsApp and are subject to their end-to-end encryption standards. We strictly do not sell, rent, or distribute your private data to third-party marketing companies.

3. Data Retention & Your Rights

Customer dispatch and invoice data is retained for up to 7 years to comply with HMRC financial auditing requirements. General inquiries that do not result in a booked service are purged after 6 months. Under UK GDPR, you retain the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us directly.

4. Cookie Disclosures

Our website utilizes static architecture and does not deploy tracking scripts, advertising pixels, or profiling cookies. The only cookies utilized are strictly operational, system-essential tags to protect our servers from automated spam and to facilitate standard routing.

Terms of Service

1. Proof of Ownership & Right to Refuse

By requesting an emergency roadside lockout dispatch, you confirm that you are the lawful owner, registered keeper, or authorized agent of the target vehicle. We operate a strict ID policy. Before any lock manipulation or bypass strategies commence, our technicians will request a valid Photographic ID and matching V5C logbook (or formal company authorization for fleet vehicles). We reserve the absolute right to refuse service and immediately vacate the premises if lawful ownership cannot be verified.

2. Non-Destructive Entry & Pre-Existing Damage

While our specialized lock-picking methods are engineered to be entirely damage-free, we accept no liability for pre-existing mechanical internal lock failures, seized wafers, deadlocked mechanism fatigue, or electrical component collapse on vehicles with compromised entry systems prior to our arrival.

3. Payment Terms & Callout Fees

Payment is strictly due in full upon completion of the requested service on-site. We accept major credit/debit cards, contactless payments, and direct bank transfers via our mobile point-of-sale systems. If a van has been mobilized to your location, a standard callout fee will apply if the booking is cancelled while our technician is actively en route, or if service is aborted due to a failure to provide proof of ownership.

4. Warranties on Keys & Programming

New transponder keys and remotes supplied and programmed by SVA Locksmiths carry a 12-month warranty covering electronic failure. This warranty is instantly voided in cases of physical trauma, casing damage, water ingress, or standard battery depletion. We accept no liability for third-party keys or parts supplied by the customer for programming.
