Account and Data Deletion

Your privacy matters to us. If you wish to delete your account and have your personal information permanently removed, please send an email to info@svalocksmiths.co.uk using the email address associated with your account and include “Account Deletion Request” in the subject line.

We will process your request promptly and permanently delete your account and personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Please note that we may retain certain information only where legally required (for example, for tax, legal, or security purposes).
