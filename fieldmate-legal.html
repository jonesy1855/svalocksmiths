Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data SVA FieldMate ("we", "us", "our", "SVA Locksmiths") collects through the SVA FieldMate mobile app ("the App"), how we use it, and the rights you have under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

We are the data controller for the personal data described below. Contact: contact@svalocksmiths.co.uk · 18 Corbet Avenue, Shawbury, SY4 4HJ

1. What we collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • When you create an account: your email address and a password (stored securely by our authentication provider — see "Who we share data with").
  • If you choose to contact us for support: your email and the contents of your message.

1.2 Information about purchases

  • The product you bought — a Pro subscription (currently £7.99/month or £79.99/year), or for early customers a one-time lifetime Pro unlock.
  • The price paid, currency and transaction date, and the entitlement we activate on your account (that you have Pro access).

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • Basic account and usage metadata provided by our hosting platform to run and secure the service (e.g. account creation time, last sign-in).
  • Nothing on the App itself is logged unless you explicitly enable it.

1.4 What we do NOT collect

  • Card numbers or bank details. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see, store or process your card data.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

1.5 Content you store in the App

  • Job records: vehicle details, registration numbers, customer names and phone numbers, job descriptions, rates, itemised prices, invoice numbers, payment status and F-Gas service records you create with the quote and job-log tools.
  • Notes and note photos: the text and photographs you save against a vehicle profile (for example a photo of a car or key you're working on).
  • Your My Kit list and stock counts (a stock count on its own is not personal data, but we list it here for completeness).
  • Messages you post in the member's Hub, together with the username and avatar shown alongside them.

This content is stored on your device and synced to your account in our Firestore database so it follows you across devices. It belongs to your account and is deleted with it (see section 6.1).

2. How we use your data

  • To create and operate your account (contract).
  • To activate and manage your Pro subscription and Pro access (contract).
  • To process a payment or refund (contract).
  • To respond to support requests (legitimate interest).
  • To keep the service secure (legitimate interest; legal obligation).
  • (Optional) To send product updates (consent — you can opt out at any time).

3. Who we share data with

  • Google (Firebase / Google Cloud): hosts your account, authentication and the Firestore database that stores your entitlements. Data is stored in the EU region.
  • Google Play: processes payments and receives your purchase details. They do not receive your Pro entitlement.
  • We only share data where necessary to provide the App, where required by law, or with your consent.
  • We never sell your personal data.

4. Where data is stored & transfers

Your data is stored with Google Cloud in the UK/EU. Where data is transferred outside the UK/EEA, we rely on the appropriate safeguards required by law (for example, the EU/UK standard contractual clauses).

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider — we never see them.
  • All communication with our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • Database access is restricted so the App can only read the data belonging to the signed-in account.
  • Only authorised administrators can access payment and audit records.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We work to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the App and meet legal obligations:

  • Your account and entitlements: for as long as your account exists.
  • Payment and audit records: as required for tax and accounting purposes (typically 6-7 years).

6.1 Deleting your data & account

You can delete your account and the personal data held on it at any time, from inside the App:

  • How to delete: open Profile (tap the account button) and choose Delete My Account. Confirm with your password and your email address. You can also email contact@svalocksmiths.co.uk with the subject "Delete my account" from your registered address instead, and we will do the same thing manually.
  • What happens: deletion happens immediately when you confirm in the App, rather than being queued for a manual request. We delete your account, your Pro access and any legacy per-make unlocks, your synced notes, photos and job records, your support message history, and any messages you posted in the member's Hub. Other members' accounts and messages are untouched.
  • What we keep: a non-identifying record that an erasure took place — the date, how many records were erased, and a one-way hash of your account identifier. It contains no email address, username, message content or photo data, and it cannot be reversed to identify you. It exists so we can evidence that a deletion request was honoured. Payment and tax records are kept separately for as long as the law requires (typically 6-7 years), as required by HMRC.
  • Device-local data: once the deletion succeeds we also clear the App's own storage on your device — cached account details, your theme and kit preferences, and any job photos taken but not yet uploaded. Anything you created after that point belongs to a new account.
  • Irreversible: there is no undo and no backup we can restore from. If you want to keep your job history, photos or invoices, export or screenshot them before deleting.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten") — including deleting your account and unlocking records. The process and what happens is explained in section 6.1 (Deleting your data & account).
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Portability of data you provided to us.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@svalocksmiths.co.uk. We will respond within the timeframe required by law (usually one month).

8. Children

The App is for professional locksmiths and automotive security technicians aged 18 and over only. It is not directed at children, and registration requires confirming you are 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe anyone under 18 has given us their data, contact us at contact@svalocksmiths.co.uk and we will delete it.

9. Cookies & local storage

The App is an offline-first application. It uses your device's local storage to save preferences and your job notes on your device only. We do not use tracking cookies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified in the App. The "last updated" date above always reflects the current version. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact
