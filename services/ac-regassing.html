AC Regassing & Climate Control

Fully automated, dealer-level air conditioning re-gassing and system diagnostics performed directly on your driveway or at your workplace.

My Air Con hasn't worked for a while, can you fix it?

Every AC service begins with a complete vacuum phase and diagnostic pressure test. If the system has a minor leak, our UV dye detection will locate it. If there is a major mechanical failure (such as a seized compressor), we will advise on the exact issue so you can consult a mechanical garage without wasting money on lost refrigerant.

Are you certified to handle refrigerant?

Absolutely. Handling automotive refrigerant requires strict environmental compliance. Our technicians hold the mandatory F-Gas certification (City & Guilds), operating to the highest disciplined standards to safely recover, handle, and recharge both older and modern vehicle systems.

System Diagnostics

  • Old Gas Recovery: Safe extraction of depleted or contaminated refrigerant.
  • Vacuum Leak Test: 20-minute deep vacuum cycle to remove moisture and test system integrity.

  • Precision Recharge: System recharged to exact factory weight specifications.
  • Anti-Bacterial Clean: Optional vent bomb to destroy mold and bacteria causing damp smells in the cabin.
